## 📌 What It Does

**AI Writing Assistant** is a Streamlit-powered web app that generates professional written content on demand. Choose your task, describe your topic, set the tone and length — and the AI does the rest.

No writers block. No agency fees. Just content, instantly.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **4 Content Types** — Product descriptions, emails, social posts, and blog intros
- 🎭 **Tone Control** — Friendly, Professional, or Casual
- 📏 **Length Control** — Choose your word count range before generating
- ⚡ **Smart Caching** — Same inputs return instant results without re-calling the AI
- 🎯 **Strict Output** — AI is instructed to never add filler or go off-topic

## 🖊️ Supported Content Types

| Task | Best For |

| **Email** | Cold outreach, follow-ups, announcements |

## 🎛️ Input Options

| Field | Options |
|-------|---------|
| **Tone** | Friendly / Professional / Casual |
| **Length** | 0–100 words / 150–200 words / 300–400 words |
| **Topic** | Free text |
| **Context** | Free text — describe what you need written |

## 🖥️ How to Use

1. Navigate to **Writing Assistant** from the sidebar
2. Select your content type (Description, Email, Social Post, Blog Intro)
3. Enter your topic and context
4. Choose your preferred tone and word count
5. Click **Enter** — your content is ready in seconds

- Word count ranges are guided, not mathematically exact
- Currently supports English only
